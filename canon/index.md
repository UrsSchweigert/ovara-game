## power

- `power.deaf-water` Deaf water: Dead water in which the gift gives no answer at all. Dead zones, the worst harbours, and any water that has been ordered.
- `power.ease` Ease: The form of intending that takes away pain and fever and gives sleep.

- `power.hold` Hold: The form of calling that makes water still and unyielding.
- `power.intending` Intending: The way of the gift that asks the sea to care. Those who practise it are healers; its forms are Mend, Purge, Ease and Restore. The result is intended water.
- `power.listen` Listen: The first form of calling: feeling what the water touches, at a distance.
- `power.mend` Mend: The form of intending that closes wounds and knits bone.
- `power.move` Move: The form of calling that pushes and pulls water.

- `power.orphan-water` Orphan water: Water cut off from the sea: tanks, pipes, bottles. The gift answers one reach weaker, and it fades fast.
- `power.purge` Purge: The form of intending that draws out poison, infection or filth, from a body or from water.
- `power.raise` Raise: The form of calling that lifts water against its nature.
- `power.reach-caller` Caller or healer: Reach 2: a boat's worth of water. About one mariner in a hundred.
