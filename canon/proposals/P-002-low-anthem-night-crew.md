# P-002: Who speaks at the night crew's hammock talk

**Asked by:** the game design (`PLAN.md`, stage 1 step 6 in `docs/design/quests.md`).

**The gap.** Stage 1, step 6 needs three topics (Jef, the Dragon, the Threaded) to be said by people of the night crew in the long dry hall at Low Anthem. Canon names the hold's night crew only as a group (`place.low-anthem`); no `person.*` entry is one of its talkers. The source (`campaign-the-man-the-tide-brought-in.md`, chapter 1) names no speaker either: "a boy says" the seed, and the hammock talk is heard "from the night crew".

**Proposal.** Three unnamed people of the night crew speak in the hammock talk: a boy (who gives the seed about the Threaded who went up to the islands), an old woman (who makes a small sign at Jef's name), and an old man (who calls Jef a clever uncle). They have no names and no history beyond being of the night crew.

**Used by:** stage 1, step 6. The three topics are heard only here, so this step must not be skipped.

**Safe fallback.** If refused: the talk is given by Davin and Sefa, who are both in canon at Low Anthem in chapter 1 (`person.davin`, `person.sefa`). Both are alive in chapter 1 (`canon/chapters/ch01.yaml`). The seed is then said by Sefa, not a boy.

**Why it matters.** An unnamed speaker of a place is allowed by `CLAUDE.md`; a name or a history is not. This proposal asks the owner to confirm that these three may speak as unnamed people of the night crew.

**Status:** waiting for the owner. Not answered.
