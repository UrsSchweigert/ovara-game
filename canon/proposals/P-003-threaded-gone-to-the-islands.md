# P-003: The Threaded who went up to the islands

**Asked by:** the game design (stage 1, step 6 in `docs/design/quests.md`).

**The gap.** Stage 1 step 6 says the night crew's hammock talk holds three topics "heard only here, including the Threaded who went up to the islands". Canon says the Threaded answer only to Jef (`faction.threaded`) and that Okonta and Corin are Threaded of the Anthem Waters; it says nothing about a Threaded who left for the islands. The source gives only the seed: a boy says one of the Threaded once went up to the islands and never came down.

**Proposal.** This is rumour, not fact. The hold tells it as a story that may be false: one of the Threaded went up to the islands and never came down. No name, no date, no reason. Canon adds nothing beyond the seed's words.

**Used by:** stage 1, step 6, as one of three topics. It is not used again in the campaign, and no flag depends on it.

**Safe fallback.** If refused: the topic is left out of the hammock talk. Step 6 is then given only two topics, Jef and the Dragon, both in canon (`person.jef`, `machine.the-dragon`). The Threaded are still introduced in step 2 of stage 1 through the Threaded topic in Sefa's talk.

**Why it matters.** A rumour about a Threaded who went to the islands touches the Threaded's rule and Jef's keeping of them. The owner should decide whether it is only a rumour or a fact the later stages must answer.

**Status:** waiting for the owner. Not answered.
