# P-005: The nursery gate and its latch

**Asked by:** the game design (stage 1, step 5 in `docs/design/quests.md`).

**The gap.** Stage 1 step 5 is a puzzle: the nursery gate latches up and across, Davin shows it, and the player must do it. Canon names the silverbacks (`creature.silverback`, the herded fish) but not a nursery, a gate or a latch at Low Anthem. The source has a "nursery terrace" with young fish and a "nursery gate", and Davin showing the latch four times (`person.davin`).

**Proposal.** Low Anthem keeps a nursery terrace of young silverbacks, closed off by a gate of stone-walled rails. The latch is a bar that is lifted and then drawn across. Only the hold's herders know it; it is shown, not explained.

**Used by:** stage 1, step 5. A failure lets the silverbacks out; Bother rounds them up (`person.bother`).

**Safe fallback.** If refused: the nursery is a pen with a plain gate, no latch to learn. Davin shows the gate in the scene and Bother's round-up stands. Canon people used: Davin, Bother, and `creature.silverback`. The puzzle becomes a plain open-and-close action, which keeps stage 1 playable.

**Why it matters.** A latch with a set order is a fact about how the hold works. It is small, but the puzzle depends on it being the same each time.

**Status:** waiting for the owner. Not answered.
