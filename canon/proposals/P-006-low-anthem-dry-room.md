# P-006: The sea-door, the dry hall and the dry room

**Asked by:** the game design (stage 1, steps 1 to 3 in `docs/design/quests.md`).

**The gap.** Stage 1 starts at the sea-door and spends three days in a dry room. Canon defines the sea-door (`canon/glossary.yaml`) and says that a hold's dry halls are where eating, sleeping and argument happen (`canon/world/physical.yaml`). It does not name the dry room where a guest is kept, nor say which room of the dome it is, nor who is in it.

**Proposal.** The dome has a dry hall for talk and sleeping, and a smaller dry room off it, where a guest is kept while the hold argues about him. The sea-door opens into the dome's floor, as glossary defines it. No name is given to the dry room.

**Used by:** stage 1, steps 1 to 3. Step 2 is the three days in the dry room; step 3 is Ione's examination.

**Safe fallback.** If refused: the three days take place in a room in the dome, with no name, and only Sefa and Ione speak there (both `person.sefa` and `person.ione`, alive in chapter 1). Sefa stands guard; Ione examines him. No other canon person is needed.

**Why it matters.** The dry room is the first room the player sees after the sea-door, so its layout will be drawn. Its name is not needed for the story.
