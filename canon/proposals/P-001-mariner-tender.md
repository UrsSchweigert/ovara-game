# P-001: What mariners trade with

**Asked by:** the game design (`PLAN.md`, "Things, money and shops").

**The gap.** Canon says islanders are paid in company scrip, and that mariners trade goods at the Wet Market and the tide line: sea-silk, pearls, smoked silverback, carved shell. It does not say whether holds use anything as money among themselves.

**Proposal.** Holds have no coin. Between holds, and with Driftfolk, value is reckoned in pearls from Pearl Stair, strung in tens. Within a hold nothing is bought: things are owed and remembered.

**Why it matters.** Shops in holds need a price. If this is refused, hold shops trade goods for goods only.
