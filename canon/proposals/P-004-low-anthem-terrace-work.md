# P-004: Cutting kelp fronds and the drying racks

**Asked by:** the game design (stage 1, step 4 in `docs/design/quests.md`).

**The gap.** Stage 1 step 4 has Wrack work a row of terrace: cut ten fronds of kelp and bring them to the drying racks. Canon has the terraces (`place.low-anthem`: "stone-walled kelp terraces all round") and the kelp (`flora.anthem-kelp`), but says nothing about cutting fronds, or about drying racks on the terraces. The novel mentions "the drying racks" on the nursery terrace without describing them.

**Proposal.** Holds dry kelp on racks on the terraces, next to the nursery beds. Fronds are cut by hand from the stone-walled rows and carried to the racks. Racks are plain frames of stone or stem; they are described no further.

**Used by:** stage 1, step 4 (Teaches carrying). Nothing else depends on the racks.

**Safe fallback.** If refused: the fronds are carried to the kelp at the terrace's edge, with no racks. The step still teaches carrying, using `flora.anthem-kelp` and `place.low-anthem`, both already in canon. No person is needed; Davin or Ione may be named as the one who sets the task.

**Why it matters.** Racks are a new piece of hold equipment. It is small, but a later stage that names it would need it to be fixed.

**Status:** waiting for the owner. Not answered.
