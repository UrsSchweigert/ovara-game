# 0004: The character system

**Context.** A review found that the hero could not overreach or grow, that weapons and turn order were undefined, that practice rewarded grinding, and that rest was free.

**Decision.** A sheet in the shape of GURPS, cut down:

- Four attributes: Body, Hands, Mind, Standing. Reach is a talent set by canon, not bought.
- Seventeen skills with five steps. Value is attribute plus step less three, against a difficulty; the margin decides.
- The gift and the craft use the margin with no dice. Weapons and dealings with people roll three seeded dice, fixed when the act is chosen.
- Safe reach is the lower of Reach and the form's step. Above it the effect scatters and costs double; above Reach, or past the ebb limit, the undertow comes.
- Points come from quest steps and stages, never from battles, and are spent with teachers.
- What the undertow takes can be asked back through Maelis from chapter 7.
- A calendar; rest costs supplies and a watch and needs safe water. No weight.
- No choice is offered that the story refuses. Standing with each people is shown, not counted.

**Consequences.**

- The hero's growth is control, as in the novel.
- Avoiding a fight costs nothing.
- Traits need canon entries and a schema.
