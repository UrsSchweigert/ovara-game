# Art guide: the working rules

This is the art director's working guide. It turns `docs/style/art-direction.md` (the owner's brief) into exact values. It must never contradict the brief; if the two disagree, the brief wins. The palette lives in `game/assets/palettes/ovara.json` and is generated by `tools/art/make_palette.py`. Do not edit the JSON by hand. Changing the palette needs the owner and a decision record.

## Five observations about Albion (text only)

Written from memory of the game's general character. Nothing here is copied, and no image is reproduced.

1. **The view is low and close.** Figures are small and sturdy, and the world is dense. Every screen has many things in it.
2. **Living against cold.** The planet's places look grown and warm; the human machinery is hard-edged and cold. The contrast tells the story.
3. **Colour is few and rich.** Shading comes from dither and hue shifts, not from many colours.
4. **The interface is a made object.** Panels look carved and bound, not drawn as flat boxes.
5. **Speech is a painted face beside the words.** The portrait carries the character.

We take these as principles only. Our shapes, colours and names are our own.

## The master palette

Hue shifts: under water, shadows lean blue-green (deep, kelp 0 to 2); on land they lean violet (stone and neon darks, rust 0). Lights lean warm (brass, rust, sand 5 to 7). There is no pure black and no pure white. Glows are lighter pixels with dither, never alpha.

## Water states

Each state differs in pattern and movement first. Every region draws all six states from its own subset; the script checks that each colour is in the subset, that deaf water is a stone grey, and that in every region the six base colours run from dark to light in the order below, each at least 20 apart in greyscale (Rec.601, 0 to 255).

| State | Pattern | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| Well | Concentric rings | Rings widen slowly |
| Storm | Streaks along the wind | Fast |
| Fouled | Diagonal dither bands, dark motes | Motes sink |
| Clean | Sparse light flecks | Flecks drift slowly |
| Deaf | None; a dotted edge where it meets other water | None at all. Never animated |
| Orphan | Fine square grid, one pixel lines every 4 | Slow slosh at the edges only |

## Outlines by faction

Outline with the darkest colour of the thing's own ramp (index 0), one pixel, and only where the figure meets a busy background. Faction is told by shape first.

- **Mariners:** long-limbed, unarmoured, cloth and cord hanging. Outline deep-0 or kelp-0.
- **Agravar divers:** squat, helmeted, blocky, straight edges. Outline stone-0.
- **Threaded:** lie along their needles, long and low. Outline deep-0.
- **The fouled:** no clear edge. No outline; a dithered rim in brass-2 and brass-1.
- **The Makers:** seamless arcs, nothing straight. Outline stone-2 on pale glass.
- **Meridian:** one grey, no rivets, no lettering. Stone ramp only.
- **Rays and Wells:** long slow shapes. Outline ember-0 or deep-0.
- **The hero:** the only figure with both: sea-silk shape, a worn tool at the belt.

## Frames and interface materials

The frame follows whose ground the chapter is on. All are drawn pixel by pixel, never smoothed, and every control is at least 44 base pixels.

| Ground | Frame | Fill | Readout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mariner | Shell and pale glass, bound with cord (sand 4 to 7, deep 5 to 7, brass 3) | deep-1 | brass-6 text |
| Maker | White metal, one inner blue line (stone-7, deep-6) | stone-1 | deep-7 text |
| Agravar | Riveted steel, rivets in stone-5 (stone 2 to 5) | stone-0 | dim green kelp-5 or amber brass-5 |
| Meridian | Plain grey, no rivets (stone-3 and stone-4) | stone-2 | stone-7 text |
| Rays and Wells | No frame; a picture panel with no words | n/a | n/a |

Dialogue panel: frame across the lower band, portrait on the speaker's side, three short lines. Text is at least 12 pixels tall. Ebb is a vessel that empties, drawn in deep and brass. Marks above speakers' heads: main quest, side quest and shop differ by shape (diamond, round, square) as well as colour.

## Portraits

- 96 by 96 pixels, master palette, head and shoulders, three-quarter face.
- One clear feature that survives being small: Jef's apron strap, Maelis's patched coat, Rook's high collar, Sefa's grey band. For anyone else, take the feature from canon's `look`; do not invent one.
- Background is a flat colour from the speaker's ground; the face is lit from the upper left.
- Faction outline rules above apply. No portrait for the rays; they show pictures.

## Fixed sizes (from the brief)

Base 640 by 360. Exploring tiles 32 by 32. Battle hexes 64 by 48. Figures 32 by 48 on the hex, anchored at the centre of the lower edge. Portraits 96 by 96. Things 24 by 24. Dungeon view 320 by 180, textures 32 by 32 or 64 by 64.

## Review

The swatch image `game/assets/palettes/ovara-swatch.png` shows the 64 colours, the five subsets, and each region's six water colours (well to orphan) beside their greys. **A person must look at it and approve the palette** before any art is made from it. No agent has looked at it.

## Manifest

Every PNG under `game/assets/` except `palettes/` is listed in `game/assets/manifest.json`: `{"assets": [{"path", "kind", "canon"?, "brief"?}]}`, with `path` relative to `game/assets/`. Kinds and sizes: `tile` 64x48, `figure` 32x48, `exploring-tile` 32x32, `dungeon-texture` 32x32 or 64x64, `thing` 24x24, `portrait` 96x96, `ui` any size. Pixels are fully transparent or fully opaque, and every opaque pixel is a palette colour. `bash ov check` fails naming the file otherwise.

Placeholders: `python3 tools/art/make_placeholders.py` writes the water tiles of every region, eight faction figures (`sprites/figure-*.png`, 32 by 48, with a two-letter label except Meridian, which has no lettering), four generic things (`sprites/thing-*.png`, 24 by 24), eight exploring tiles (`tiles/explore-*.png`, 32 by 32: floor, wall and the six Anthem water states) and four Maker dungeon textures (`tiles/maker-*.png`, 32 by 32: wall, floor, ceiling and door, stone-7 with deep-6 arcs), and their briefs and manifest entries, and `shots/placeholders.png` (a contact sheet of every manifest entry) for the owner to approve. It is deterministic; rerunning changes nothing.
