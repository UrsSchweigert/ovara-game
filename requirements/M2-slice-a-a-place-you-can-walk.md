# Milestone 2: slice A, a place you can walk

Low Anthem on screen. See `PLAN.md` section 2 (Exploring, Talking, Mobile first) and `docs/style/art-direction.md`.

Read with `PLAN.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and, for anything about quests or places, `docs/design/quests.md`. Lanes and path limits are in `CLAUDE.md`, "Working under the harness".

| ID | Requirement | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **M2.1** | **Exploring scene** | The exploring mode: 32-pixel tiles, standing sprites in row order, tap and hold to walk, camera in whole pixels, placeholder art. |
| **M2.2** | **Dialogue and topics** | The dialogue panel with portrait, three lines at a time, and the topic list. |
| **M2.3** | **Journal, party and pack screens** | Each usable with one thumb. |
| **M2.4** | **A shop** | One stall at Low Anthem, trading goods for goods. |
| **M2.5** | **Cut-scene player** | Runs the step lists from content; the arrival scene of stage 1; skippable. |
| **M2.6** | **Saves** | Automatic, on every change of area and every quest step; resume in place. |
| **M2.7** | **Builds** | Android and iOS export presets and the steps to build each, written down. Signing material never enters the repository. |

## Gate

The owner plays chapter 1 start to finish on a real phone and says the look is right.

## Needs the owner

- Every judgement of how it looks and feels: workers cannot open a window. Expect a manual test for each scene.
- Installing export templates, the Android SDK and Xcode; signing; running the builds on phones.
